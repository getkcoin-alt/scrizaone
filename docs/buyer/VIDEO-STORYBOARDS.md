# Recording plans — scripts, not published videos

Use real people/recordings, captions, a reviewed transcript and the date/version shown. TranscriptCard is an accessible text-first component; no empty video player or fabricated recording is published.

| Explainer | Suggested shots and spoken sequence | Evidence/approval gate |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-meeting questions | Open the local brief; name the problem and intended user; compare one alternative; show the open questions and proposed agenda. | Match current UI; no actual customer details. |
| Cost explanation | Show two similar forms with different approval/integration complexity; explain cost drivers and ongoing operations; invite a written scope. | No approved numeric pricing model exists; do not put invented prices on screen. |
| Fintech walkthrough | Create sample request, receive success, replay duplicate, introduce report mismatch, reconcile sample report. Explain the operator decision. | Prominent synthetic-data label; no bank access or compliance claims. |
| Software walkthrough | Submit sample enquiry, fail calendar connection, operator accepts in the sample, cancel; show both perspectives. | Scheduling state is simulated. Do not show it as a live appointment. |
| Organic service walkthrough | Choose a buyer question, prepare an answer, connect the tool, review the enquiry path and unproven-demand failure. | No invented traffic or revenue charts. |
| After an enquiry | Show review and consent, an isolated mock acceptance, the separate delivery/booking concepts, and truthful failure/export alternatives. | Use mocked transport; do not email the team merely for recording. |
| Genuine customer journey | Draft only: business problem -> actual implementation -> observed outcome -> limitations -> client words. | Blocked until permissioned evidence and measurement exist; no invented testimonial. |
| Actual team introduction | Draft only: real role -> relevant responsibility -> how the person contributes -> how the team handles unknowns. | Blocked until person, biography, consent and real footage are supplied. |
| Supporting a company claim | Tie one specific claim to a reproducible product operation or primary record and state its limits. | Company-published identifiers or certifications need source/scope verification; a demo alone cannot substantiate them. |

Public transcript-ready scripts exist on cost, prototype and meeting-process resources. They are explicitly labelled recording scripts, not recordings.
