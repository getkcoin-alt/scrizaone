## Riza behaviour

The visitor chooses Guide me, Riza. All guidance state is memory-only and resets on reload. Pointer presence over a marked section can prioritize that section locally; no coordinates or pointer history are stored. Marked section visibility must persist at least 6.5 seconds; at most two automatic suggestions per page and five per visit, at least 60 seconds apart. Suggestions pause while typing, chatting, in hidden tabs or while the consent banner is visible. There is no cursor trail, session recording, fingerprint or inferred private conversation. A referrer is reduced to search, AI referral, internal, other or unknown locally. Exact search queries are not available.

Show me where moves focus to the real target, briefly highlights it and flies the avatar toward it. The reduced-motion preference removes the flight. Nothing is clicked automatically. Ask about this pre-fills a short public-context question, which the visitor reviews and sends themselves. Suggestions never send reading activity to a model. Chat retains the existing disclosure and request limits. Analytics records only allowlisted coarse events after separate analytics consent; no calculator values, brief text, contact data or raw URLs.
